# Armory

**Armory** is about making weapons feel like they belong to your character.

In vanilla Minecraft, your sword only exists when it's in your hand. The rest of the time it's an icon in a menu. Armory changes that idea: your weapon has a place on your body. You carry it on your back where everyone can see it, draw it when you need it, and sheathe it when you don't.

## The idea

Armory is building toward a true armory: a collection of weapons that each feel physical and personal.

Not just more swords with different damage numbers, but weapons with presence.
